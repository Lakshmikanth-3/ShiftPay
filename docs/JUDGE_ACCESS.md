# Judge access

No accounts, passwords or test logins are needed. Each app creates its account from a passkey on your device
(Face ID, fingerprint, Windows Hello or a phone).

| App | Link | Best on |
|---|---|---|
| Employee wallet | https://shiftpay-employee.vercel.app | A phone (installable) |
| Business payroll | https://shiftpay-business.vercel.app | A laptop |

## Try the employee side in two minutes

1. Open the employee link, type a name, tap **Create payroll passkey**, approve.
2. Wait a few seconds: ShiftPay tops up a small network-fee credit for every new account automatically.
   Then tap **Get AUSD**: Agora's testnet faucet sends 10,000 test AUSD.
3. **Send** → *Abroad · instant settlement* → any address → 1 AUSD. The transfer settles through Agora's
   Instant Settlement pair and shows the transaction link.
4. **Profile → Save today's payslip**, then open the same link in another browser with the same passkey and
   **Open payslips**: the encrypted payslip decrypts there too.

## See payroll end to end

The business account is the team's company passkey, so judges watch payroll in the demo video, or ask the team
for a live session: register your employee address, start a shift, and watch your wages grow every second.

## Stateless test

Clear site data or open a fresh browser, then unlock with the same passkey: the account, the linked payroll
(found from the passkey's address on chain) and the private payslips all reconstruct. Nothing needs to be restored.
