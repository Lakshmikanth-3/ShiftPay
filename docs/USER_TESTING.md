# User testing kit

Run this with at least five people who have never used ShiftPay, ideally people who work hourly or part-time.
Do not help them unless they are stuck for more than 30 seconds; note where they got stuck.

## Setup (organiser)

1. Business site: https://shiftpay-business.vercel.app, company passkey unlocked, USDC funded.
2. Give the tester a phone with https://shiftpay-employee.vercel.app open.

## Tasks for the tester

| # | Task (read aloud) | Record |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Create your ShiftPay account." | Seconds to finish; any confusion |
| 2 | "Show me your account so your employer can pay you." (Receive) | Did they find it? |
| 3 | Organiser registers them and starts a shift. "Tell me how much you have earned." | Did they understand the number? |
| 4 | "Take out some of what you earned." | Seconds; did they expect a fee? |
| 5 | "Send 1 dollar to this address, as if to family abroad." | Seconds; confusion points |
| 6 | "Close the browser, open it again and get back in." | Did the passkey restore everything? |

## Questions after the tasks (1 = no, 5 = yes)

1. Did anything make you think "this is crypto"? What?
2. Would you use this if your employer offered it?
3. How often would you take out wages before payday?
4. Who would you send money to, and how do you send it today?
5. What would stop you using it?
