# Demo script (about 2 minutes 50 seconds)

Use the live sites and real Monad testnet transactions only. Show each transaction on the explorer.

| Time | Screen | What to show and say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Title | Shift workers wait two weeks for money they already earned. ShiftPay pays wages as they are earned, funded by the employer, with no borrowing. |
| 0:20–0:45 | Business site, sign-in | Unlock the company Mera passkey: no wallet extension, no seed phrase. Overview shows funded payroll, earned wages and tax escrow. |
| 0:45–1:10 | Employee mobile app | Employee creates a passkey on the phone and copies the address from **Receive**. |
| 1:10–1:30 | Business → Employees | Add the employee with rate, withholding and allocation; show the confirmed registration and funding. |
| 1:30–1:50 | Business → Shifts | Start a verified shift. On the phone the earned balance grows; tax is held separately. |
| 1:50–2:10 | Employee → Withdraw | Withdraw earned wages. The employer's relayer pays gas; show the transaction. |
| 2:10–2:35 | Employee → Send / Cash out | Get AUSD from Agora's faucet, send AUSD to another address, then cash out through Agora Instant Settlement; show the hashes. |
| 2:35–2:50 | Repository | Contract addresses, tests, trust assumptions and what remains. |
